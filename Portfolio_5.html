Research & Publications

Under Process - IEEE Transactions
Solid-State Marx Generator with a Novel Passive Gate Driver Topology
Abdul Rehman, Umer Farooq, Ali Haider, Tanveer Abbas

This paper details a novel architectural approach to extreme-voltage pulsed power. By engineered a robust Passive Gate Drive Circuit (PGDC), the design achieves true modular scalability up to 44 stages, entirely eliminating the volumetric and financial burden of providing isolated auxiliary active power supplies to every floating node. Performance was validated via empirical hardware testing, successfully producing a 30.4 kV output pulse with a compressed 380 ns rise time using standard, commercially available IGBTs.
